01
Foundations

The IB Model, Explained

Before you promote anything, you need to actually understand the machine you are plugging into. An Introducing Broker (IB) is a person or entity that refers clients to a forex or CFD broker and gets paid a share of what the broker earns from that client's trading, for as long as that client keeps trading. It is one of the oldest, most legitimate distribution models in the retail trading industry, used by everyone from one person YouTube channels to full prop trading academies.

Where the money actually comes from

Brokers make money on the spread (the small gap between buy and sell price) and or a commission per lot traded. When you introduce a trader, the broker shares a slice of that spread or commission with you, not with the trader's losses or wins. This distinction matters enormously, both ethically and legally:

Get this exactly right

You are paid from trading activity (volume), never from a trader's losses. You have zero financial incentive for anyone to lose money, and you should never imply otherwise, to a prospect, a regulator, or yourself. If a program ever pays out based on client losses rather than volume, that is a serious red flag and not something to build a business on.

How the Base IB rebate works

Base IB pays on a rebate basis, also called revenue share: a set amount per lot traded, or a percentage of the spread, for as long as your referred trader keeps trading. There is no one time payout just for a sign up. A referral starts earning you money once they open a funded account and place real trades, and it keeps paying for the life of that trading relationship, not a single upfront moment.

Why this beats "just signals"

Signals and calls are a service. The moment you stop posting, the income stops. An IB structure is a business asset: once someone is trading under your link, you continue earning from their activity whether or not you post that day. Layer a Sub IB network on top (Module 03) and you are earning from other people's networks too. That is the entire reason this course exists, to move you from "creator with an audience" to "owner of a distribution network."

Module 01 takeaways

  • You earn from trading volume, never from client losses.
  • Base IB is the network layer sitting on top of our broker partners' IB programs.
  • This is a compounding, ongoing asset, not a one off payout for a post.

Navigating PU Prime

The walkthrough below covers PU Prime specifically, since it is the most common starting point. IC Markets and Vantage follow a similar sign up and transfer process through their own apps. There are two different paths depending on whether someone is brand new to PU Prime or already has an account.

Brand new to PU Prime

They register through your personal referral link or referral code (both live on your IB dashboard, covered below). They land under you automatically the moment they sign up, no extra form needed.

Already has a PU Prime account

They do not need to open a second one. Instead they transfer their existing account's IB connection over to you. The account needs a minimum balance of £300 in it, and they must not open any new trades until the transfer has fully gone through: open positions or trading activity mid transfer can cause it to fail.

Step 1
From their profile page in the PU Prime app, under "Other," they tap Transfer IB/CPA.
Step 2
Partnership Type: IB. New CPA/IB: your rebate account number. They tick both confirmation boxes (positions closed, agree to transfer all trading accounts) and hit Submit.

A client account can only be connected to one affiliate at a time, so the transfer has to fully complete before anything else happens. If it is rejected or fails, it cannot be resubmitted for 72 hours, so get the details right the first time.

03
The Multiplier

The Sub IB Layer

This is the module that separates a Base IB partner earning a modest side income from one running a real network. A Sub IB is someone you bring into the Base IB network who runs their own IB business underneath yours. They get their own link, their own dashboard, their own referred traders, and you earn an override commission on their network's activity, on top of your own direct commissions.

Why this is the actual growth lever

Your personal time to post content, message prospects, and follow up is finite. A Sub IB's time is not yours, but their production still pays you. Ten Sub IBs, each modestly active, will out earn one person grinding solo, every time. This is the same duplication principle behind every durable referral or affiliate network: your income scales with the size and activity of your network, not the size of your personal effort.

Single tier vs deep networks

Keep this simple at first. Focus on recruiting Sub IBs directly under you (one tier) until you have three to five consistently active ones. Only once you have proven you can support that many should you start coaching your best Sub IBs to recruit their own, because at that point you are not just building a network, you are training network builders, and quality control gets harder the deeper it goes.

How this actually works inside PU Prime

When you connect a Sub IB, you set their rate yourself, at whatever level you choose below your own. For example, if you earn $10 per lot, you might set them at $5 per lot instead. They earn on every client they bring in and get trading; you earn the gap between their rate and yours on top of every lot those clients trade, automatically, without doing any of the legwork yourself.

04
Growth Engine

Finding & Attracting Traders

Growth in this business is not "post and pray." It is a repeatable funnel: attract attention with real value, earn trust, then make the invitation. Skip the first two steps and every invite reads as a pitch.

Know exactly who you are talking to

  • The aspiring full timer: trading part time, wants to go pro, hungry for structure and mentorship.
  • The lost beginner: has lost money following random signals, wants someone honest to follow.
  • The sideline watcher: follows trading content but has not opened a live account yet, needs a reason and a safe first step.
  • The creator trader: has their own small following and could become a Sub IB rather than just a trader.

Every piece of content and every message should have one of these four people in mind. Generic content attracts generic, low intent, attention.

The value first funnel

1. AttractFree, genuinely useful content: market breakdowns, risk management lessons, real trade recaps (wins and losses).
2. TrustConsistency and transparency over weeks. Show your process, not just your P&L. Answer questions in comments and messages.
3. InviteA natural, low pressure offer to join under your Base IB link, framed as "here's how you can trade alongside the community," not "sign up now."
4. SupportOnboard them properly (Module 06) so they actually start trading. An unfunded referral earns nobody anything.

TikTok Lives: your fastest community builder

Nothing builds trust and a sense of "real community" faster than a TikTok Live: people watching you read the market in real time, asking questions, and getting to know you as a person, not just a clip in their feed. It is also one of the few formats where a viewer becomes a follower and a community member inside a single session.

Consistency beats everything else

The single biggest lever on TikTok Live is showing up at the same time, every single day. The algorithm rewards a predictable schedule with more FYP placement, and your audience builds a habit around it. Both only compound if the time does not move around. A live, off the cuff format is exactly where "just this once" financial advice slips out. Everything you say on a live session is educational and analytical only: reading price action, explaining your own process, reacting to news, never a direct instruction to buy or sell. Say what you see and what you personally are doing, not what the viewer should do.

Actually going viral: reverse engineer, don't guess

The most reliable way to go viral is not waiting around for inspiration. It is finding formats that have already proven they work and putting your own twist on them.

  1. Collect ten videos that have already gone viral on TikTok or Instagram, in trading or an adjacent niche. Save them somewhere you can find again.
  2. Break down why each one worked: the hook, the pacing, the format, not just the topic.
  3. Rebuild it with your own twist: your setup, your voice, your take, the same proven structure underneath.

Use Instagram trial reels to test before you commit

Trial reels only get shown to people who don't already follow you, so you can test a video's hook and pacing against genuine strangers before deciding whether to share it to your main grid. It's one of the fastest ways to find new leads who have never seen you before, without risking your existing following's feed with something that flops.

A simple script for explaining Base IB

You will be asked "what is this, how does it work" constantly. Have one honest, repeatable answer ready, do not improvise it each time:

Template

"I trade through [broker], and I'm part of the Base IB network. It just means if you open an account through my link, you get [the same conditions / your standard perks], and I earn a small share from the broker on the trading activity, at no extra cost to you. It's how creators like me get paid instead of charging for signals directly."

Do's and don'ts

  • Do show real results, including losing trades and drawdowns.
  • Do disclose the referral relationship plainly, every time.
  • Don't promise or imply guaranteed profits, "risk free" trading, or specific returns.
  • Don't edit or fabricate P&L screenshots. This destroys trust permanently and can end an IB partnership outright.
  • Don't pressure anyone financially insecure into opening an account they cannot afford to fund.

Conversion

Converting Followers into Partners

Content brings people close. This module is about the actual conversation, turning an interested follower into someone trading (or building their own Sub IB business) under you.

The invite framework

  1. Ask, don't announce. "Are you trading live yet, or still building it up?" opens a real conversation instead of a pitch.
  2. Match the offer to what they said. A beginner gets the "trade alongside the community" framing; a creator trader gets the Sub IB framing (Module 03).
  3. One clear next step. Never end a conversation without a specific action: "here's the link, message me once you've signed up and I'll get you set up in the group."
  4. Follow up once, generously. A single, no pressure check in a few days later ("no rush, just checking if you had questions") converts more than any hard close.

A short swipe file for the first message

Use these as starting points, not scripts to copy word for word. Swap in your own voice:

Opening a warm conversation

"Hey! Saw you've been watching the lives, appreciate you being around. Are you trading live yet or still building things up on your side?"

Inviting a beginner

"No pressure at all, but if you ever want to trade alongside the community you can use my link to get set up, same conditions as anyone else. Happy to help you get started whenever you're ready."

Inviting a creator trader as a Sub IB

"You've got a solid little following building. Ever looked into the Sub IB side of Base IB? You'd run your own link and earn from your own audience, and I'd walk you through the whole setup personally."

Gentle follow up

"No rush at all, just circling back in case you had any questions about getting set up. Here if you need anything."

07
Compounding

Scaling the Network

This is where most people plateau, not from lack of effort, but from trying to do everything personally forever. Scaling means teaching duplication, not doing more yourself.

Duplication over addition

Every time you personally onboard a new trader, that is addition. Every time you turn a strong trader into a Sub IB who then onboards their own traders, that is duplication, and duplication compounds while addition does not. Your job past a certain point is to become a teacher of this exact course to your best people, not just a top of funnel content machine.

A simple network rhythm

  • Weekly: a short check in message or call with active Sub IBs, what's working, what's stuck.
  • Monthly: a network wide review, top performers, dashboard numbers, one thing to improve.
  • Quarterly: revisit this course with your Sub IBs, what's changed, what to double down on.

Recognition costs nothing and retains everything

People stay in networks where they feel seen. A public shoutout for a Sub IB's first funded referral, a simple leaderboard, or an informal "mentor" tier for your most active partners will retain people far more effectively than any commission bump.

The over recruiting trap

Bringing in ten Sub IBs and supporting none of them produces ten inactive links and a lot of resentment. Only recruit as many Sub IBs as you can realistically onboard and check in with (Module 06). A smaller, active network always out earns a large, dead one.

09
Not Optional

Compliance, Ethics & Longevity

This module isn't optional reading, it's what protects your income, your reputation, and the ability to keep running Base IB at all. Every broker relationship depends on partners staying inside these lines.

Always

  • Disclose that you earn from referrals, plainly, not buried in fine print.
  • Include a risk warning wherever you talk about trading results: trading forex and CFDs carries a high risk of loss and isn't suitable for everyone.
  • Follow the specific promotional rules your broker's IB agreement sets out (some restrict paid ads, specific claims, or platforms, know yours).
  • Keep your own trading and marketing records honest: real screenshots, real numbers.

Never

  • Guarantee profits, a specific return, or "risk free" trading.
  • Pressure someone into depositing more than they can afford to lose.
  • Use fake or embellished P&L, fake testimonials, or borrowed screenshots.
  • Promise Sub IB commissions or tiers that aren't actually confirmed in writing by Base IB.
Why this protects you

An IB relationship can be terminated by the broker for compliance breaches, instantly ending the income from every trader in your network, not just the one violation. Treat every post as something that has to hold up months later, because it will still be online months later.

This is a long game. The IBs who are still earning in five years are the ones who built trust slowly and never burned it for a short term spike. Play it that way from day one.

Glossary

IB (Introducing Broker)
A partner paid a share of broker revenue for referring trading clients.
Sub IB
An IB recruited and supported by another IB, sitting one tier below them in the network.
Override commission
The share an upper tier IB earns from a Sub IB's network activity, on top of their own direct commission.
Rebate / rev share
Ongoing commission paid per lot traded or as a percentage of spread, pays for as long as the client trades.
Lot
A standardized unit of trade size; commission is commonly calculated per lot traded.
Funded account
A trading account that has deposited real capital and is eligible to trade.
Churn
Referred traders or Sub IBs who go inactive, the main enemy of long term IB income.
Downline
The full set of traders and Sub IBs (and their own recruits) sitting underneath you in the network.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a big following to start?

No. Your first referrals almost always come from direct conversations with people you already know, not viral reach. Content builds the long term engine, but it isn't the required starting point.

Am I responsible for my referrals' trading results?

No, and you should never suggest you are. You're responsible for honest information and fair support, not for anyone's individual trading outcomes. Trading carries real risk that sits with the trader.

How is a Sub IB different from just referring another IB?

They're the same starting action: you refer someone into the Base IB network as a partner rather than a trader. The difference is what happens after. You support them (Module 07) and earn an override on their entire downline, not a one time referral.

What if a referral stops trading?

This is normal churn. It's exactly why Module 08's tracking and Module 06/07's check ins matter, catching disengagement early and re-engaging is far more effective than only chasing new sign ups.

Can I run paid ads to promote my link?

Check your broker's specific IB agreement first; many restrict certain ad platforms, claims, or keyword bidding on the broker's own name. [confirm current Base IB / broker ad policy]
